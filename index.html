Trust & verification

Trust is built with people in the loop.

Creator Finder uses clear confirmation steps and careful human review. Verification is not an automatic approval promise.

Sign up with email to begin your account request.

Enter a one-time verification code sent to your inbox.

A profile review is completed over several days by the team.

Public Instagram, YouTube, and X profiles can be checked for authenticity signals.

Creators can share their Creator Finder ID through an authentic social account.

Approved profiles receive a trust badge; suspicious profiles may be rejected after review.
